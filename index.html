About the Festival

The RRIS Festival is an annual celebration of creativity, talent, and academic excellence organized by Ram Ratna International School and Ram Ratna Prathama Pre-School.

This inter-school festival brings together students from various institutions to showcase their abilities across multiple domains including cultural performances, literary arts, academic exhibitions, and performing arts.
